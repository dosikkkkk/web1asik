Nikola Tesla

1856 – 1943 | Pioneer of Alternating Current & Electrical Engineering

About Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor, electrical engineer, mechanical engineer, and futurist best known for his contributions to the design of the modern alternating current (AC) electricity supply system. Born in Smiljan, Austrian Empire, he later emigrated to the United States where he pioneered groundbreaking electromagnetic inventions.

Life Timeline

  1. 1856: Born in Smiljan, Austrian Empire (modern-day Croatia).
  2. 1884: Emigrated to the United States to work with Thomas Edison.
  3. 1888: Sold patents for alternating current motors to George Westinghouse.
  4. 1891: Invented the Tesla Coil, widely used in radio technology.
  5. 1943: Passed away in New York City at the age of 86.

Key Inventions & Contributions

  • Alternating Current (AC) electrical supply system
  • Induction motor and rotating magnetic field principles
  • Tesla Coil for high-voltage wireless power delivery
  • Early developments in radio control and wireless communication
